Analysis of TSPO PET-based WM lesion phenotypes according to location in arterial vascular zones (watershed, mixed and non-watershed): 

Pipeline components are as follows: 
1) PET_SUVR_generation - generation of the [11C]PBR28 PET SUVR maps
2) Watershed_Mask_generation - generation of the binary mask of arterial vascular zones in MNI152 space using the Digital 3D Brain MRI Arterial Territories Atlas by Liu et al (2023) (DOI: 10.1038/s41597-022-01923-0)
3) TSPO_PET_WML_phenotyping_analysis - statistical analyses by arterial vascular zone and TSPO PET WML phenotype
